Who We Are

Hope for Children Foundation is a non-profit organization dedicated to supporting vulnerable children through education, healthcare, food assistance and community development.

Our Mission

To improve the lives of vulnerable children by providing quality care, education and opportunities for a brighter future.

Our Vision

A world where every child grows in a safe, healthy and loving environment.

Our Core Values

  • Love
  • Integrity
  • Compassion
  • Teamwork
  • Accountability

Our Programmes

Programme Description
Education Support Providing school fees, books and learning materials.
Healthcare Providing medical care and health awareness.
Food Support Providing meals to vulnerable children and families.
Child Protection Ensuring children grow in safe and secure environments.
